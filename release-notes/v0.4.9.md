## What's Changed

### Binary Size Diet, Dead-Code Cleanup & MCP/Parser Hardening
- **~43% Smaller Release Binaries**: Enabled `opt-level = "s"` and `panic = "abort"` in `[profile.release]` (`bitefoundry-obsidian.exe` reduced from ~17.6 MiB to ~10.1 MiB; zip archive ~7.7 MiB to ~5.0 MiB) with zero feature loss.
- **Dependency & Dead-Code Diet**: Removed 36 crates from `Cargo.lock` (unused `futures`, `uuid`, `notify-rust`, `rustls`, `hex`, `urlencoding`, dead `src/ui/notification.rs`, unused `crossterm/event-stream` and `reqwest/stream`), moved `tempfile` to dev-dependencies, upgraded `ratatui` to `0.29` and `dirs` to `6`, trimmed `bitefoundry-html` `resvg` features, and added optional Cargo feature gates (`html-js`, `svg-graphics`) for lightweight headless builds.
- **Safety & Docs**: Hardened projected-read `unwrap()` sites in `src/mcp.rs` and parser state transitions in `src/vault/parser.rs`, synchronized `docs/openapi.yaml` version via `scripts/sync-version.mjs`, resolved all Clippy warnings (`-D warnings` clean), and externalized `assets/demo.gif` to `bitefoundry.dev`.
