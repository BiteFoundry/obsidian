## What's Changed

### Modular Architecture & Terminal Safety
- **Terminal & Agent Safe-Output (`src/safe_text.rs`)**: Sanitized untrusted vault note titles, paths, tags, tasks, and snippets rendered to CLI terminal output against ANSI/VT100 escape sequences (CSI/OSC/DCS/C1), C0/C1 control characters, Unicode Bidi overrides, and invisible Unicode Tag characters (`U+E0000..=U+E007F`), while preserving raw `--json` output.
- **Supply Chain & Packaging**: Added `[package.metadata.binstall]` for `cargo-binstall`, `deny.toml` + `.github/workflows/audit.yml` (`cargo-deny` CI), and `refs/tags/v` tag guards on publish/release jobs.
- **Codebase Modularization**: Decomposed `src/cli.rs`, `src/storage/db.rs`, `src/ui/app.rs` (`dispatch_core` 1,095L → 88L), and `tests/vault_e2e.rs` (8,628L → 10 domain submodules) into cohesive modules.
